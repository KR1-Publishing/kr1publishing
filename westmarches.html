The playstyle

One world, and whoever turns up

A West Marches campaign has one world and many players, but no fixed weekly group. Players say when they are free, and whoever shows up that night forms the party.

Every session starts in town and ends back in town, or it does not end at all. The party sets its own goal, marches out and comes home with a map, a rumor or a dead friend. The next group walks into whatever this one left behind.

The name comes from a campaign Ben Robbins ran and wrote about on his blog, Ars Ludi. I wrote the Guidebook after running my own, because I wanted the whole method in one short book I could hand to another GM.

How a night runs

How a session usually runs

1

The town board

Rumors, open jobs and old maps hang on a board in town where everyone can read them, and the players choose what they want to chase.

2

The goal

Before anyone leaves town, the party agrees on a goal they can say in one sentence, and the GM only has to prepare for that.

3

The march

The party heads out into the wilderness, where the danger grows the further they go, so the players decide how far they dare to push.

4

The report

Back in town the map is updated and new rumors go up on the board, which gives the next group something to chase.

Rumor board, Thornwatch

  1. The ferryman will not cross after dark. He will not say why.
  2. A second party found the old watchtower empty. The first one did not come back.
  3. Someone is paying in silver for wolf pelts. Lots of them.

Questions

Before you buy

Which game system does it work with?

They work with any system, because they describe how to run the campaign and leave the rules to you. They sit fine next to Shadowdark, OSR games, 5e, Pathfinder 2e or whatever you already play.

Do I need all four books?

You can start with the Guidebook alone, since it holds the whole method. The other three are working books for the GM, the group and each player. If you want everything, the West Marches Playkit bundles all four.

Is there something free I can try first?

Yes. The West Marches Expedition One-Sheet is free. One page per expedition: who marched out, what they were after, what happened and what came home.

I want lots of tables. Is this the right book?

The Guidebook is about how to run the campaign, and some readers wanted more tables. If that is you, pair it with the Sandbox Playstyle Toolkit: The City for the home base and the Hexcrawl series for the wilderness.

Is it available in print?

Yes. The PDF came out in May 2025 and print followed in July 2025. All four books are print on demand from DriveThruRPG, in softcover and hardcover, and several game shops in Scandinavia stock them. Shops can order them at retailer terms on the vendor page.

Is it available in other languages?

The Guidebook is out in Italian on DriveThruRPG and in Spanish from El Refugio de Ryhope. A Polish edition is on its way.

Was AI used to make it?

I wrote the books myself. AI tools were used as a final proofreader for typos and inconsistencies. The text, art and layout are made by people.
