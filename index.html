The obvious questions

Why give away the method?

Because I struggled for two decades, and I really do not want you to do the same. I want to see more independent publishers selling their games, starting with you.

Does this only work for tabletop games?

The sequence is general. Every example is from my own catalog, so if you publish for tabletop it will feel familiar immediately, and if you do not you will need to translate a few specifics.

How often do you email?

The first two weeks, a short series that walks you through the method behind the tools you picked. After that, only when there is something to say. Usually a launch, a number that surprised me, or a mistake worth warning you about.

Do I need the Blueprint to use the checklist?

No. The checklist works on its own and always will. The Blueprint is for when you want the reasoning behind each box, not just the box.
