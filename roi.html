Why this book

The die has already been rolled

In 2024 Karl Otto began a blog post about AI in roleplaying games, and never published it. The debate felt too heated, and even asking open questions could feel like taking a side.

After twenty-five years in the hobby, and two games made without any AI, his writing time had shrunk to lunch breaks, plane rides and the hours after the children were asleep. That was when he started to experiment.

The book is the result: a calm look at earlier shifts like the printing press and photography, at what the tools can and cannot do, and at the judgment and responsibility that stay with the person holding the pen.

Questions

Before you buy

Is it for or against AI?

Neither. It does not try to settle the debate. It looks at earlier shifts in technology, what the tools can and cannot do, and why human judgment stays at the center of creative work.

Who is it for?

RPG writers, designers and GMs who feel conflicted, curious or uncertain, and want a calm place to think about AI in creative work.

How long is it?

Ten short chapters, an introduction and an epilogue, meant to be read in one or two sittings.

Was the book written with AI?

Yes, with AI support, and the epilogue explains how. Some sections started as notes organized with AI, and some parts were written with AI and then revised or rewritten. The structure, the tone, the message and the personal stories are the author’s own.

Is it available in print and audio?

Yes. It is a black and white softcover, print on demand from DriveThruRPG, and there is an audiobook as well. Shops can order the softcover on the vendor page.
