The game

Your diploma is your first step out the door

You play young graduates fresh out of the Academy. They leave home for the first time and take on quests and errands to prove themselves. Some come back with a name. Some come back with a story.

Your diploma decides who you are and what you can do, from Loremaster and Channeler to Smith and Globetrotter. Every skill you learn grows from it.

All you need is the book, two six-sided dice per player and a notebook.

How it plays

Fast to learn, built to tell stories

Roll two dice

Tale is Powered by the Apocalypse. Roll 2d6 when it counts. A 10 or more is a clean success. 7 to 9 gets it done at a cost. Less, and the story turns against you.

A diploma, not a class

Each graduate carries a diploma with its own actions, skills and qualities. Twelve ready diplomas from the Academy of Merelin get a new group playing in minutes.

Quests and errands

The world sends work your way. The Quest Pack gives you a full campaign of quests, errands and arcs to run straight from the page.

The story

From backer to partner

Tale was created by Hans Olav Ekornrød and published by Game Forlag in 2019, after a successful Kickstarter.

Karl Otto from KR1 Publishing was a superbacker, and bought a game session with Hans Olav and Thomas from Game Forlag. Seven years later the two publishers have joined forces to give Tale its second wind.
