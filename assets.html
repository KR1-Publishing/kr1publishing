Who you're stocking

KR1 Publishing

A tabletop RPG publisher based in Kongsberg, Norway, run by Karl Otto Kristoffersen together with a network of authors, illustrators and partner studios. The West Marches Guidebook is the best seller with 4,450+ copies sold, and the Hexcrawl series (written with Matthijs Holter) has three books in print and 6,500+ copies sold. In Geek Native's 2025 DriveThruRPG publisher league table, KR1 finished eighth, one place behind Cubicle 7 and two behind Onyx Path.

Logo files (PNG and SVG) are in the ZIP for use on your shelf talkers, web listings or social posts. Full story at karlottokristoffersen.com.

Whatever you carry out is your start-up capital, and one final dice roll decides whether the company survives. A5, full colour, public domain art.
