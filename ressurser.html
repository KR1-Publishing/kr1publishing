Can you run one open campaign for more players than fit at your table?

The West Marches Campaign Playstyle Guidebook is a system-agnostic GM guide to the West Marches style of play: a player-driven campaign where different groups explore the same wilderness on different nights, and the world stays consistent across all of them.

Instead of one fixed group and a plot you steer, this book shows you how to set up a campaign that thrives on exploration and player choice, from the first session to a shared world with years of history.

Whether you run Shadowdark, OSR, 5e, Pathfinder 2e or another fantasy RPG, the method adapts to your system.

Inside you'll find:
- The six core principles of West Marches play
- How to prepare one session at a time, for whoever shows up
- A safe home base, a dangerous wilderness and how the two connect
- Running the game so players set the goals and the pace
- Sharing discoveries between groups so the world grows
- Character-driven storytelling without a script

Perfect for GMs who want more players than one table can hold, a world that outlives any single party, and a campaign that runs on curiosity.

Does your world explain itself, or does it only make sense in your notes?

The Hexcrawl Worldbuilding Design Guidebook is a system-agnostic GM guide for building hexcrawl regions, hex maps, settlements and factions that connect to each other. Every hex points to at least one other: what caused it, or what it causes.

Whether you run Shadowdark, OSR, 5e, Pathfinder 2e or another fantasy RPG, these tools adapt to your system while strengthening the foundation of your hexcrawl: the world your encounters sit in.

- The fact, clue and mystery structure for ruins, and what lives there now
- Printable cards for terrain, settlements, factions, ruins, history and growth

Can your Company secure enough capital to survive?

Gründer is a business adventure RPG for 3 to 6 players. You found a Company together with fellow ambitious minds and set out on a daring adventure to secure the funds you need. Through risky decisions, clever planning and cooperation, you chase opportunity in a world where profit always comes at a cost.

The game is built for fast, focused play and works especially well as a one-shot. Sessions end in a final roll that decides the fate of the Company: success, failure, or something in between.

Rules from Into the Odd, in the same family as Cairn, Mausritter and Mythic Bastionland. Inspired by OSR design principles and historical business realities.

What still depends on human creativity when the tools change under you?

Roll for Innovation is a short reflective essay about AI and creativity in tabletop roleplaying games. It began as a blog post in 2024, when the conversation around AI felt too heated to be useful. This book is an attempt to slow things down and think more clearly.

It does not argue for or against AI. Instead it reflects on judgment, responsibility, and what still depends on human creativity.

Inside you'll find:
- Ten short chapters, from Fear of a Black Dragon to Enter the Dragon
- Reflections on judgment and responsibility in creative work
- What the tools change, and what they do not
- Questions for your own practice as a writer, designer or GM

Perfect for RPG writers, designers and GMs who feel conflicted, curious or uncertain, and want a calm place to think about AI in creative work.

Is your drawer full of finished games that nobody has read?

I Just Sold 10K Books (and you can too) is a beginner's guide to selling on DriveThruRPG. Between 2019 and 2024 the author sold sixty-nine books on the storefront. Then one release in May 2025 moved two hundred and fifty copies in its first week, and by June 2026 he had passed ten thousand books sold.

Instead of a theory of marketing, this book gives you the order things were done in. Four phases, and selling is the last one: make one small finished thing for one real person, give it away, grow by running the loop, then sell once the value is obviously bigger than the price.

Inside you'll find:
- The four phases, step by step
- Key highlights behind ten thousand sales, from the author's own account
- Why selling comes last, and what happens when you start there
- The honest timeline, flat years included
- A one-page checklist to print and tick off as you go

Perfect for makers with years of work in the drawer and no audience yet. About thirty pages, short enough to read in one evening.
