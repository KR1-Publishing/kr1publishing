What the numbers actually mean

A badge is not vanity. It is placement. Once a title carries a metal it shows up in more lists, and lists are where strangers find you. That is why a cheap book that sells steadily can outperform an expensive one that sells in a single burst.

The gap between Copper at fifty one and Gold at five hundred and one looks enormous from below. It is roughly one steady year at fifty copies a month, and most of that comes from the books you launch afterwards pulling the earlier ones along with them.
