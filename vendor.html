Silverheart: The Legacy of the Deep

The roleplaying game from the silver mining town of Kongsberg, made for the town's 400th anniversary with support from the Municipality of Kongsberg and expert assistance from the Norwegian Mining Museum. The game was gifted to the town's secondary schools, is used in teaching today, and is sold in the museum shop.

Recommended retail $45. Institutional pricing and class sets on request. An English edition and a school portal with ready made lesson plans arrive in 2027. Read more about Silverheart for schools.
